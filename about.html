About Skyra
We didn't reinvent aviation. We just finally built it for people.

Skyra was built on a simple truth: millions fly daily, yet passengers are clueless about their rights, families are left waiting in the dark, and pilot fatigue goes undetected. Aviation has always worked for airlines. We built Skyra to make it work for people.

We're not reinventing aviation — we're showing the right data in the right place. Combining existing flight APIs, public ATC recordings, and AI to give passengers and crew the intelligence they've always deserved.
